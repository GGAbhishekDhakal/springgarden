About Us

Bring Fun Life To Your Kids

Spring Garden Kids World is a friendly early-learning school in Basundhara, Kathmandu. We believe every child deserves a happy, safe and creative place to grow.Our classrooms are filled with fun, our playgrounds are safe, and our teachers are caring. We follow a holistic approach that develops young minds and builds strong character.
